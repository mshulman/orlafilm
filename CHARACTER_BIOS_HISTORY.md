## 2. Extended Character Bios (from `js/app.js` History)

### **Orla**
> "Orla is a bright but deeply troubled 14-year-old girl who has recently lost her mother. She finds it impossible to settle into her home and school life with this new reality. She believes if she can escape to Ireland, her birth place, she will find comfort and solace with her relatives. She works day and night to put everything in place for her journey."

### **The Stranger**
> "The Stranger is a refugee in his early 30s. He recently arrived in the county after being the sole survivor of a terrible accident at sea. A stranger in a strange land who came ashore with a special mission, he is searching for his community and his audience. Orla believes that she sees him perform miracles."

### **Orla’s Dad**
> "Dad is barely holding it together. Recently widowed and grieving deeply, he has the responsibility of caring for his two daughters, Orla, 14, and Lily. By day he is trying to be a good father, but by night the sadness moves in and he drinks to ease his profound sorrow."

### **Lily**
> "Lily is the bridge between Orla and Dad. Although they are completely at odds with one another, they both agree that Lily needs their loving care."

### **Orla’s Mum**
> "Mum comes to the story only through Orla’s memory of her. She feeds Orla’s internal conflict and ultimately becomes the inspiration for Orla’s redemption."

### **Fantasmo the Elephant**
> "Fantasmo the Elephant becomes a magical force in Orla’s life, as she sees part of herself in the eyes of this magnificent animal, and wants to liberate her from the chains that bind her."
