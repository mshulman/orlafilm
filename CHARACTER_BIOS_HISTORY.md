# Character Bios — *ORLA*

This document contains the character card bios for *ORLA*, reflecting the refined British English copy.

---

### **Orla**
> "Orla is a very smart but deeply troubled 14-year-old girl who has recently lost her mother."

---

### **The Stranger**
> "The Stranger is a mysterious dark-skinned, dark-haired refugee in his early 30s."

---

### **Orla’s Dad**
> "Dad is barely holding it together. Recently widowed and grieving deeply, Dad has the responsibility of caring for his two daughters."

---

### **Lily**
> "Lily is the bridge between Orla and Dad, who are completely at odds with one another."

---

### **Orla’s Mum**
> "Mum comes to the story only through Orla’s memory of her, yet she is a pivotal character."

---

### **Fantasmo the Elephant**
> "Fantasmo the Elephant becomes a magical force in Orla’s life, as she sees part of herself in her eyes."
