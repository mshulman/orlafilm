IMPACT / MISSION

A film designed to start conversations and social engagement.

ORLA combines audience appeal with the potential to open meaningful conversations around grief, belonging, displacement and human connection.

The project also offers the potential for an Impact Campaign developed alongside the film’s release. With grief, redemption and the troubled teenage experience at its heart, ORLA creates natural opportunities to engage with wider conversations around young people, loss and connection. The Stranger’s experience of displacement provides a further avenue for meaningful social-impact engagement.
